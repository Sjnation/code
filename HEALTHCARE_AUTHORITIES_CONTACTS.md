## 📝 **HOW TO APPLY**

### **Step 1: Choose Authority & Role**
- Visit the careers portal for each authority
- Search for "Program Coordinator", "Customer Experience", or "Administrative Coordinator"
- Filter by: Vancouver or Lower Mainland region

### **Step 2: Prepare Application Materials**
- ✅ Resume (PDF format preferred)
- ✅ Cover letter highlighting:
  - 4+ years project coordination experience
  - Stakeholder management skills
  - Customer satisfaction improvements
  - Communication strengths
  - Healthcare sector interest (if applicable)

### **Step 3: Submit Application**
- Use online portal when available
- Follow specific application instructions
- Keep copies of applications for tracking

### **Step 4: Follow Up**
- Wait 1-2 weeks after application
- If no response, send polite follow-up email to recruitment contact
- Example: "Hi, I applied for [Position] on [Date]. Please let me know about the status."
