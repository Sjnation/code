# 🎯 CONVERT YOUR RESUME TO PDF - 3 EASY METHODS

## **METHOD 1: Browser Print-to-PDF (⭐ FASTEST - 30 SECONDS)**

### Steps:
1. ✅ Open the file: `resume.html` in any web browser
2. ✅ Press: **Ctrl+P** (Windows) or **Cmd+P** (Mac)
3. ✅ Click: "Save as PDF"
4. ✅ Name it: `Shubham_Jain_Resume.pdf`
5. ✅ Click: "Save"

**Done!** Your resume PDF is ready! 📄
